# Curriculum

`CurriculumWrapper` raises difficulty when a moving-average reward clears a threshold.

| Phase | Hosts | Scenarios | Mechanics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Novice | 5 | ransomware | static, 3× reward |
| Intermediate | 25 | + apt | DHCP every 80 ticks, 1.5× |
| Expert | 100 | + iot_grid | DHCP 40, dynamic topology, 1× |
